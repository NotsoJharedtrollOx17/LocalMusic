# Local Music v0.10.0

27 October 2026. Reviewed personalized playlists, integrated locally through `dev` into `main`.

## Included

- The playlist creator offers Familiar, Balanced and Explore mixes using eligible listening history, metadata and available source-audio similarity. A cold start can use favorites and filters or explore the local library.
- Sound analysis is local, silent and explicitly initiated by generating a playlist. It uses bounded excerpts and cached descriptors, without adding listening history. Availability and coverage are shown in the creator; unavailable analysis falls back to other signals.
- Up to three artists can be included and three excluded. Included artists are mixed across available tracks; exclusions take priority. When artists are selected, genre is a preference; otherwise it is a filter. Other selected restrictions still apply.
- Suggest less and Undo give reversible track-level feedback. Generated previews remain stable until regenerated or saved. Saved playlists can be renamed or deleted from their menu. The four automatic collections and saved playlists have separate headings.
- The generator reports its current analysis/matching stage. Its loading indicator rotates more slowly under Reduce Motion.

## Validation and distribution

See [verification](../verification.md) for actual release checks and package evidence. Recommendation quality requires listening review. Android runtime/background behavior, physical-device performance, battery and acoustic-analysis acceptance remain separate gates.

This is a local annotated release with development packages; it is not production-signed hosted distribution. Earlier releases, plans, recordings and reference images are preserved.
