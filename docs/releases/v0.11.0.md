# Local Music v0.11.0

27 October 2026. Reviewed focus and Your Recap improvements, integrated locally through `dev` into `main`.

## Included

- Rounded, theme-aware keyboard highlights and icon hover outlines, with complete frames around library entries and navigation tabs. Focused tabs scroll fully into view.
- Your Recap exports show the recorded top songs, artists and albums with song and album artwork. Story and Feed use consistent, compact vertical rankings.
- Equal play counts rank the most recently played song, artist or album first. Existing snapshots retain their recorded order.

## Validation and distribution

See [verification](../verification.md) for release checks and package evidence. Android runtime, physical-device and manual accessibility acceptance remain separate gates.

This is a local annotated release with development packages; it is not production-signed hosted distribution.
