Moving from an earlier Android debug build?

The production app has a permanent release signature, so it cannot update a debug-signed installation in place. Back up and review your app data before a clean installation. The release guide explains the transition.

A PERSONAL PROJECT

Built by Abraham
Jhared Flores Azcona.

I wanted a player for the music I already own, with some of the character I missed from older players. The visualizers, the orange dot, the little listening rituals—Local Music grew from there.

Your music and listening history stay on your device. There are no accounts or analytics here. The code, methods and test record are open, too.
